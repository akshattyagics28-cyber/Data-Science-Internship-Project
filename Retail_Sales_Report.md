# Retail Sales Analysis Report

## Business Objective
Analyze sales data to identify product, region, and time-based performance patterns. Use the cleaned data to predict future order sales.

## Dataset
- **Clean records:** 720 retail orders
- **Time period:** January 2024 to December 2025
- **Fields:** date, region, product category, units sold, unit price, discount, sales, profit, and customer rating

## Findings
1. **Total sales:** $548,617.55.
2. **Total profit:** $141,999.34.
3. **Top sales category:** Electronics ($339,968.32).
4. **Most profitable region:** North ($36,473.23).
5. **Seasonality:** sales are stronger during mid-year and year-end promotional periods in the data.
6. **Sales drivers:** units sold, unit price, discount, category, and region are suitable inputs for a sales-prediction model.

## Conclusion
Retail sales can be improved by tracking high-performing categories, supporting profitable regions, and monitoring the impact of discounts. The notebook provides a reproducible workflow for ongoing analysis and sales prediction.
